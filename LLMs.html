Tokenisation

This is the first step and many of you maybe already familiar with this concept. It is the mapping of words into an a series of numbers called token. There are various libraries that have methods defined that can turn your words into tokens. One popular library is the transformers library which allows us to use tokenisation methods from open source models. 


from transformers import AutoTokenizer

tokenizer = AutoTokenizer.from_pretrained("bert-base-uncased")
text = "Hello, how are you?"
tokens = tokenizer.tokenize(text)
